## Ingredients

- 1 block tofu
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/2 cup nutritional yeast
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp steak spice

## Method

1. Press your tofu for at least 15 minutes, but the longer the better!
2. Use cornstarch in your seasoning!
3. Air fry & flip halfway through cooking
4. You can bake as we it will just take a little longer - about 30 min as opposed to 15 in the air fryer
5. This tofu steak recipe is one of my go to’s! I eat it at least twice a week & it’s super simple to make!✨
6. Tofu Steak Ingredients✨
7. Slice your extra firm pressed tofu into strips
8. Add soy sauce to one bowl & seasonings to another.
9. Dunk your tofu in the soy sauce then into the nooch mixture.
10. Air-fry @ 400f for 14-16 minutes flipping halfway though! Or bake @ 400 for 25-30 min flipping halfway.
11. Thank you for watching & enjoy!
