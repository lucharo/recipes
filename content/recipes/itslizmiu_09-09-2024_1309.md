## Ingredients

- 1 cup Greek style soy yogurt
- 1 cup soaked cashews
- 2 tsp salt
- 4-6 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 2 tbsp tapioca starch
- 1 tbsp agar
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tsp miso paste
- 2 tsp dijon mustard
- Juice of 1 lemon
