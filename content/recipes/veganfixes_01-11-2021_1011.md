## Ingredients

- 2 cup water
- Seeds from half a honey melon

### Steps

- 1. Scoop out the seeds from the melon.

## Method


### Steps

1. Add the seeds to a blender along with water. Blend for 2 minutes.
2. Strain using a cloth, and enjoy!

## Notes

Don´t throw away your melon seeds! Make the sweetest melon milk instead🤤
