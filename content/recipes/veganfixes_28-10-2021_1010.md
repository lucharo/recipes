## Ingredients


### Serves

- 4 as a side, 2 as a mains
- 1 large cauliflower, remove florets, leaves and core
- 1-2 tbsp olive oil
- 2-3 tbsp cornflour or cornstarch
- Salt
- Black pepper

### Tahini Mustard Lemon Sauce

- 1 tbsp tahini
- 2 tsp dijon mustard
- 1/4 lemon, juice
- 1 tsp maple syrup

## Method


### Tahini Mustard Lemon Sauce

1. Preheat oven at 200C or 390F
2. Add the cauliflower, leaves and the core to a large mixing bowl. Pour the olive oil and mix. Next, add the cornflour, salt and black pepper and mix again.
3. Spread on a baking sheet evenly and bake for 15-20 minutes until the cauliflower is cooked and the edges begin to brown.
4. Meanwhile, to make the sauce, add all the ingredients to a empty jam jar or bowl, shake or whisk until smooth.
5. Add the baked cauliflower and leaves to a bowl, drizzle the tahini mustard lemon sauce and mix well.
6. To serve, add cauliflower to a plate or bowl, sprinkle a bit of sesame seeds and chives.
7. You can have this by itself, next to any dish or incorporated into tacos, pasta and rice dishes.
8. Enjoy!
