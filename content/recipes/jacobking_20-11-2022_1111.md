## Ingredients

- Kale
- Butternut squash
- Beetroot
- Pomegranate seeds
- Handful of pecans

### For dressing

- 1/2 cup tahini
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1/2 clove garlic
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Chop and roast squash (roast for around 30 minutes @ 200° with a little oil
2. massage the kale with oil and leave to rest/soften for 10/15 minutes.
3. add the pecans and squash seeds to the oven (to toast them) half way through cooking the squash.
4. mix the kale, chopped beetroot and roasted squash then top with pomegranate seeds, roasted squash seeds and pecans. Stir in and top with some dressing and enjoy!
