## Ingredients


### RECIPE (1-2 servings, 45min prep time)

- 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed.
- Rub them fry with a kitchen towel.
- 1-2 Tbsp oil
- salt to taste
- 1 Tsp cumin and paprika
- 1/2 Tsp garlic and chili powder

### RECIPE (8 muffins, 2h prep time)

- 3 cups (375g) all purpose flour
- 2 Tsp sugar
- 2 Tsp instant yeast
- 1 Tsp baking powder
- a pinch of salt
- 2 Tbsp vegan butter or oil
- 3/4 cup + 2 Tbsp (200ml) lukewarm water

## Method


### RECIPE (1-2 servings, 45min prep time)

1. Mix well and spread on a baking tray.
2. Bake at 200C/400F for 35mins.
3. Turn off the heat and keep them in the oven for 10 more minutes (for an extra crunch).
4. Devour immediately.
5. ENGLISH MUFFINS 🍞
6. Making English muffins at home is much easier than I thought and they turned out SO FLUFFY! 😍
7. Does anyone know why they’re called „muffins“? 🤔

### RECIPE (8 muffins, 2h prep time)

8. Knead until it’s a soft dough (takes about 5mins).
9. Let it rest for 1 hour.
10. Shape into 8 balls.
11. Let them rest for 40mins.
12. Flatten each ball.
13. Fry on medium heat 4-5mins each side until fluffy.
