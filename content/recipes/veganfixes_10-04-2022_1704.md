## Method

1. Place the dried aromatics into the bowl first (hot oil may overcook these) so they don't turn bitter
2. I used two types of chili flakes for texture and spice level
3. Used oil that can withstand high heat. If you prefer more oil than chili flakes, please adjust the amount accordingly.
4. Make a flavored oil if you like - add bay leaf, star anise, or cinnamon
5. I hope you give this easy chili oil a try. Have a beautiful weekend!
