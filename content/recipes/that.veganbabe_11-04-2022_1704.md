## Ingredients

- 4 frozen bananas
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 1/2 scoop vegan vanilla protein powder
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- 3/4 cup almond flour
- 3 tbsp almond butter
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp vegan chocolate chips 🍫

## Method

1. First blend up the ingredients for your banana ice cream! I used my #NinjaBlender to get the creamiest output!
2. Next blend up the ingredients for the cookie dough using the small cup attachment and stir in your chocolate chips.
3. Break up the cookie dough into bite sized pieces and mix it into your nice cream.
4. Top with extra cookie dough and chocolate chips and enjoy!!
