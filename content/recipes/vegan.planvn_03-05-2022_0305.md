## Ingredients

- 3 minced garlic cloves
- 1/4 inch minced ginger
- 1 medium spiced chilli
- 1 diced carrot
- 1/4 diced white cabbage
- 1 block firm tofu
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sriracha
- 3 spring onion
- rice paper sheets
- cornflour slurry (stir in a few tbsp of cornflour to your water)

## Method

1. start by frying the garlic, ginger and chilli until soft then add the carrot, cabbage and tofu then fry for another few minutes
2. now add the soy sauce and sriracha then fry for another few minutes before stirring in the spring onion and leaving then leaving the mixture to cool down.
3. now dip your rice paper sheet into your cornflour/water mix then place a big spoonful of filling in the centre and fold into a square (see video).
4. wrap the dumpling with another dipped rice paper sheet so they don’t split and then fry each side in hot oil until crispy.
5. serve with dipping sauce of choice and ENJOY! 💕
