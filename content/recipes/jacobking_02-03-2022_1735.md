## Ingredients

- ramen noodles to serve 2
- 1/4 inch minced ginger
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 1/2 tbsp miso paste
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 1/2 tbsp mirin
- 1/2 cup vegan milk (oat or soy works best)
- 1 cup water
- spring onion
- sesame seeds
- firm tofu (baked or fried till crispy)
- beansprouts

## Method

1. start by frying the garlic, ginger + chilli flakes on a low/medium heat for around 5 minutes.
2. add the miso paste, tahini, mirin + soy sauce then combine into a paste.
3. add the water + oat milk then simmer on a low heat for another 5 minutes, I then strained the broth to make it smoother but you don’t have to.
4. place some cooked noodles in a bowl then pour over the broth then add the cooked tofu, sweetcorn, beans sprouts + spring onion.
5. top with sesame seeds, chilli oil + sesame oil then serve + enjoy 😊

## Notes

This dish has got everything you could want; creamy, spicy goodness with noodles, protein + fresh veggies 😯

Super simple recipe and you can use up any veggies you may have in your fridge, enjoy + get creative with this one!
