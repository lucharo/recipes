## Ingredients

- 12 mini tortillas
- 1/2 onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 small green pepper
- 1 small orange pepper
- 400g extra firm pressed tofu
- 1/2 cup fire roasted tomatoes (or tomato sauce)
- 3 tbsp taco seasoning
- Handful of cilantro
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/2 cup raw cashews
- 1/4 cup water
- Avocado Cilantro Lime Dip
- 1 avocado
- 1 jalapeño, deseeded
- Handful cilantro
- 1/4 cup hemp seeds
- Juice of 1-2 limes
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 3-4 tbsp water

## Method

1. Sauté your onion until translucent then add in your garlic & bell peppers.
2. Shred your tofu & add it into your veggies along with some taco seasoning & fire roasted tomatoes.
3. Make a cashew cream by blending together your cashews & water. If you do not have a high speed blender make sure to soak your cashews first!
4. Mix in your cashew cream & add in some chopped cilantro & fresh lime juice.
5. Add the filling to mini tortillas and roll them up nice and tight!
6. Add your taquitos to a greased baking dish, brush with some olive oil and bake at 400f for 25-30 minutes until golden.
7. Serve & enjoy!
