## Ingredients

- 70g aquafaba
- 240g gluten-free plain flour
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed and 1 ripe banana for decoration
- 20g sunflower oil
- 60g vegan butter, melted
- 120g light brown sugar
- Zest of lemon
- 2 tsp almond extract (or vanilla extract)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp mixed spice
- 50g walnuts, optional
- 90ml plant-based milk
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

## Method

1. To make the vegan buttermilk:
2. Stir the ingredients together and sit for a few minutes.
3. Pre heat oven to 175C (350F)
4. Whisk the aquafaba for 3-5 minutes until it’s foamy and volume increases.
5. In a mixing bowl, add the oil, butter and sugar whisk to combine. Next, add the sugar mixture to the whipped aquafaba, almond extract, vegan buttermilk and 3 mashed bananas. Then combine the flour, baking soda, salt, lemon zest and walnuts fold.
6. Pour the mixture in a lined loaf tin and carefully lay the sliced banana on top with a sprinkle of brown sugar. Bake in the oven for roughly 45-50 minutes.
7. Allow to cool before slicing the banana bread.
8. Enjoy!
