## Ingredients

- 125g silken tofu
- 1/4 tsp black salt (sub for regular salt if you don’t have black salt!)
- 1/2 cup chickpea flour (40g)
- 1/2 cup water (125ml)
- 1/8 tsp bakingpowder (1g) (not soda!)
- less than 1/8 tsp turmeric (OPTIONAL – it’s just for colour and doesn’t make a huge difference)
- Vegan butter, for frying

## Method

1. If its looking a little too flimsy to flip after you fold the corners in, just turn the heat down and let it sit there to cook together and really get that structure a bit more
2. a lot of the egginess can actually cook-out of the black salt so if you like em REAL eggy like I do, I also like to sprinkle on black salt after cooking!
3. Black salt (kala namak) is a volcanic rock salt with a pungent, sulfurous aroma, commonly used in South Asian cuisine to add an umami, egg-like flavour. I stan.

🍳 Silken tofu caries in water content brand to brand so you might have to adjust the water slightly but you’ll nail it

🍳 First timers, make sure your pan isn’t too hot and be generous with the butter!!
