## Ingredients

- 10 medjool dates
- 1/4 cup natural peanut butter
- 2 tbsp peanuts
- 1/3 cup melted vegan chocolate I use @enjoylifefoods chocolate
- Salt

## Method

1. Slice the dates halfway and remove the pit.
2. Fill with natural peanut butter and some peanuts (alternatively use crunchy peanut butter)
3. Drizzle with melted chocolate and a generous amount of salt (the sat really makes it taste like a snickers bar so don’t omit!)
4. Freeze or place in the fridge for about 10-15 minutes for the peanut butter and chocolate to set!
5. These are life changing!!!
