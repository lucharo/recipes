## Ingredients


### For the ice cream

- 350 g vegan whipping cream
- 2/3 cup almond butter (recipe on feed!)
- 1/4 cup dates syrup
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 300 g coconut condensed milk
- 100 g dark chocolate

### For the caramel sauce

- 1/3 cup almond butter / peanut butter
- 1/3 cup dates syrup / maple syrup
- 3 tbsp coconut oil

## Method

1. Make the ice cream:
2. Whip the vegan whipped cream until it reaches stiff peaks. In a different bowl, mix the coconut condensed milk, almond butter, dates syrup and salt until smooth. Add 1/3 of the whipped cream to the condensed milk mixture and fold using a spatula. Pour the condensed milk mixture into the whipped cream and fold again to combine. Add the chopped dark chocolate and fold it in. Pour into a container and freeze overnight or for several hours until solid.
3. Make the caramel sauce:
4. In a bowl, mix all the ingredients until smooth and shiny.
5. ENJOY🧡
