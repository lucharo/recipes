## Ingredients

- 3 cups cashews
- 2 cups water
- 1-2 cloves of garlic
- 1-2 tbsp hot sauce (I used @sauce )
- salt and pepper to taste (I’m generous with the salt)
- you can also add nutritional yeast or cheese, or a squeeze of lemon for more flavor

## Method

1. soak cashews for an hour so they’re easy to blend, then combine ingredients and enjoy 🙂
