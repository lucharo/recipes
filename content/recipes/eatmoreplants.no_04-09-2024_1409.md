## Ingredients

- 250 g red split lentils
- 400 ml (1.5 cup) cold water
- 0.5 tsp salt & pepper
- 0.5 tsp onion powder

## Method

1. If you want to try @veri you can get a 20% discount with my code: VSM-EATMOREPLANTS
2. Recipe (6 wraps)
3. Rinse the red lentils a few times in cold water.
4. Soak the lentils in cold water for at least two hours or overnight if possible
5. Add all the ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth.
6. Heat a non-stick pan. Cook for approx. 3 minutes before flipping and cooking for another 3-4 min.
