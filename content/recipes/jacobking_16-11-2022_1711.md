- 1 can jackfruit
- 1 cup stock (I used oxo vegan chicken stock)
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/4 cup cornflour
- pinch of salt + pepper
- 1/2 cup soy milk
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tsp chilli paste
- 2 or 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp gochujang paste
- sesame seeds/spring onion for garnish
