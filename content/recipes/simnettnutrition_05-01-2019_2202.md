## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp tamari
- 1 tsp of hot sauce (or more)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp of onion powder
- 1 tsp grainy mustard
- 1 tsp of maple syrup (optional)

## Method

1. Mix all these together and marinate tempeh overnight or press it into tempeh with your fingers (or back of a spoon) if you do not want to marinate it overnight. Bake at 375 F for 10-12 minutes.
