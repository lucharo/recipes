## Ingredients

- 500ml plant based cream, we used @oatly whippable 🫶
- 400g pistachio butter
- 90g sugar
- 150ml plant based milk of your choice

## Method

1. Mix all the ingredients together until smooth & freeze for 4-5 hours, it’s so simple 🙌
