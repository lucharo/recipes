## Ingredients


### RECIPE (4 small jars, 20min prep time)

- 450g cookies (I recommend speculoos)
- 5 Tbsp almond butter
- 2/3 cup (160ml) plant milk

### RECIPE (6-8 Crêpes, 20min prep time)

- 1 cup (125g) flour
- 1 Tbsp corn starch
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1 1/3 cup (325ml) plant milk

## Method


### RECIPE (4 small jars, 20min prep time)

1. Blend until it’s a fine powder
2. Blend again until smooth.
3. It’ll last in the fridge up to 2weeks.
4. EASY VEGAN CRÊPES 🥞
5. I love Crêpes and this is my easy, foolproof recipe for having perfectly soft Crêpes everytime. 😋
6. You can fill them with a chocolate spread, cinnamon sugar or anything else you like. 🙌

### RECIPE (6-8 Crêpes, 20min prep time)

7. Mix together until it’s a smooth batter.
8. Spread in a non stick pan a thin layer of the batter.
9. Fry each side for just 1min until golden brown.
10. Enjoy warm.
