## Ingredients

- 3 cups oats
- 1.25 cups pumpkin purée
- 1/3 cup maple syrup
- 1 tbsp pumpkin pie spice

## Method

1. Mix until combined then separate into 8-10 cookies & bake for 15 minutes @350F
2. Enjoy & thank you for watching!

## Notes

🎃These fall inspired cookies are made with only 5 ingredients and are perfect for meal prep!
