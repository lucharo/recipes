## Ingredients

- 4 large potatoes, I used Maris Piper, sliced into thin discs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp harissa paste

## Method

1. Sprinkle of oregano
2. Sprinkle of chilli flakes
3. Salt and pepper to taste
4. Preheat oven to 200c
5. In a large bowl add your sliced potatoes
6. Into the bowl add your oil, harissa paste, oregano, chilli flakes, salt and pepper.
7. Mix well so the potatoes are coated
8. Place them evenly on to a prepared baking tray.
9. Roast for 20-25 minutes or until crispy and golden, turning halfway
