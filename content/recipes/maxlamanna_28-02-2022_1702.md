## Ingredients

- 250g/2 cups AP flour (or gluten-free flour)
- 530ml/ 2 1/4 cup oat milk
- 30g/2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp cinnamon

### FAKE nutella

- 60g dark chocolate
- 65g peanut butter or hazelnut butter

## Method


### FAKE nutella

1. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients until smooth and there are no flour clumps.
2. To a non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat, pour the batter, then rotate it to spread the batter as thinly as possible.
3. Cook for 2-3 minutes until slightly brown on one side and flip. Cook for another 1 minute.
4. FAKE nutella:
5. Warm dark chocolate and peanut butter, stir until smooth and creamy.
6. Spread fake nutella and fold the crêpe, add your favourite toppings.
