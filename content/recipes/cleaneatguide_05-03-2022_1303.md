## Ingredients

- 1 cup cooked chickpeas
- 3 tbsp peanut butter
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 1.5 tsp vanilla
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 2 tbsp vegan chocolate chips
- Flakey salt for topping

## Method

1. Blend your chickpeas, peanut butter, maple syrup & vanilla until smooth.
2. Add in your baking powder & soda and blend for 20-30 seconds.
3. If your cookie dough is warm let it cool for 5 minutes in the freezer, otherwise the chocolate chips will melt.
4. Mix in your chocolate chips & place into a greased skillet. Top with more chocolate & flakey salt.
5. Bake @350f for 15 minutes until the top is golden
6. Top with vegan ice cream & enjoy!!

## Notes

👌The perfect healthier dessert option with only 8 simple ingredients! The dough can also be eaten raw! 🤤
