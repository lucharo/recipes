## Ingredients

- 100% you need to give this a go 🤘😍
- 1 Block of silken tofu
- 3 Tbsp Soy sauce
- 1/2 Tbsp Rice Vinegar
- 1 Tbsp Brown Sugar
- 1 Tsp savory all purpose seasoning
- 2 Cloves garlic
- 1 Thumb piece of ginger
- 1 Tsp Chilli Oil
- 1 Tsp crispy Chilli oil for the top

## Method

1. Serve with rice your choice of veg
2. Pickled ginger and sesame seeds and spring onions
3. Boil some water and steam your tofu and veg for 8 mins.
4. Put your rice on the boil and cook to packet instructions.
5. Now mix together the sauce ingredients and shake well.
6. Once tofu is steamed, drain the water and pour over the sauce ingredients.
7. Serve up the rice and top with the silken tofu and more of that sauce and the veg and sesame seeds and enjoy!
