## Ingredients

- 2 cups (5 dl) almond flour
- 1 cup (240ml) yogurt
- I brushed mine with coconut oil and sprinkled with sesame seeds + salt

## Method

1. If you want to try @veri you can get a 20% discount with my code: VSM-EATMOREPLANTS
2. Mix the almond flour and yogurt together in a bowl.
3. Knead for 3-5 min and form into a long log.
4. Slice the log into 6 bagels. Poke a hole in the middle.
5. Brush with coconut oil and sprinkle with seeds + salt or «everything but the bagel seasoning»
6. Bake 200C/390F for 15-20 minutes.
