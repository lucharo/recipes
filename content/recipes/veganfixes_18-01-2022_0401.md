## Ingredients

- 400g of canned chickpeas
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp Paprika powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp corn starch

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F (230 degrees C).
2. Blot chickpeas with a paper towel to dry them.
3. In a bowl, toss chickpeas with olive oil, corn starch and season to taste with salt, paprika powder, and black pepper.
4. Spread on a baking sheet, and bake for 20 to 30 minutes, until browned and crunchy. Watch carefully the last few minutes to avoid burning. (They can be kept in a sealed jar for around 3/4 days in the refrigerator)

## Notes

A protein-packed vegan snack! You can enjoy them on their own,

or add them to your favourite dishes for a crispy texture.
