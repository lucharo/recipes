## Ingredients

- 300 g (whole wheat) pasta
- 1-2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion
- 3-5 cloves of garlic
- 1 tbsp parsley stems
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1/2 cup (80 g) cashews
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) veggie broth
- 4 tbsp nutritional yeast, optional for cheesy flavor
- juice of 1-2 lemons to taste
- fresh herbs (I used parsley and basil)
- salt and pepper

### Optional to serve

- vegan parmesan
- toasted nuts
- fresh herbs
- more veggies or chickpeas
- 601 kcal
- 25 g protein
- 57 g carbohydrates
- 77 g fats
- 13 g fiber

## Method

1. Soak the cashews in hot water for at least 10 minutes.
2. In a pan sauté the onion until translucent, add the garlic, parsley stems and lemon zest and cook for a few more minutes. Deglaze with a splash of lemon juice and release all the flavors from the pan.
3. In a blender or food processor blend the ingredients for the sauce.
4. Pour sauce back to the pan and add some fresh herbs such as parsley or basil.
5. Cook pasta according to packaging until al dente. Save some pasta water.
6. Add cooked pasta to the sauce, mix well and add pasta water until it reaches your desired consistency.
7. Try it yourself and tag me @herbifoods ✨ I just love seeing your remakes so much!
8. Add more veggies or chickpeas to increase the nutritional value of this meal
9. NUTRION (per portion, without toppings)
