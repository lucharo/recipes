## Ingredients

- 40 gr oats
- 150 ml plant milk
- 50 ml coffee (or dairy-free chocolate milk)

### Topping

- vegan yogurt
- dark chocolate
- cocoa powder

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients for the oatmeal together. Heat it up in a pan for about 4 minutes.
2. Top with toppings.
3. Enjoy!
