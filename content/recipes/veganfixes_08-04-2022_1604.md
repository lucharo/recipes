## Ingredients

- 250g firm tofu, press out the excess water
- 100g frozen corn
- 50g chopped chives

### Seasoning

- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp mushrooms powder
- 2 tbsp rice flour
- 1 cup bread crumbs
- Oil for frying

### Dipping sauce

- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp chopped scallions
- 1/2 tbsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 2 tbsp hot oil
- 1/2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp black vinegar

## Method


### Dipping sauce

1. Mix well & ready to serve
2. ) In a mixing bowl add all the ingredients and seasoning. Then add in the rice flour and mix till well combine.
3. ) Make mixed ingredients into small balls.
4. ) Coat the tofu balls with bread crumbs and set aside.
5. ) Heat oil and deep fry the tofu balls in medium low heat until golden brown.
6. ) Serve along with your favourite veggies and dipping sauce.

## Notes

Crispy and crunchy on the outside and soft on the inside. This meatless meatballs are a perfect plant-based alternative to your favourite snack! It is vegan and easy to make. Serve this along with your favourite veggies and dipping sauce.

Prep time: 10 minutes

Cook time: 10 minutes

Make 15 meatballs
