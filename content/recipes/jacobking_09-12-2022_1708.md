## Ingredients

- 6 carrots
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp caraway seeds
- 2 tbsp tahini
- juice of 1 lemon
- olive oil for roasting (2 tbsp for dip)
- 2 tsp harissa (2 separate tsp)
- fresh parsley
- black sesame seeds to garnish

## Method

1. peel the carrots and roast them in the spices listed in the ingredients. Roast for around 30mins-1hr at 180°C
2. blend the roasted carrots up, along with the juice of a lemon, tahini some oil to combine it all
3. put this in a serving dish then top with a drizzle of harissa and olive oil mixed together
4. finish with fresh chopped parsley, black sesame seeds and enjoy!
