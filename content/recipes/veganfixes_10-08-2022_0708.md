## Method

1. If you don't have a steamer, don't worry, you can wrap in a foil, then bake until tender or check out my other ways to cook the eggplant on my blog
2. The sauce is the same one that I used for my vegan salmon bowl but you can also use the vegan unagi made from tofu and potato sauce
3. If you are not a fan of eggplant and miss the unagi, google 'woonheng vegan unagi eel'
4. and if you are an eggplant fan and love to buy them in bulk when they are on sale, try this recipe. I usually prep them ahead of time, steam, then cut open and store them in a container. When ready, I will then sear and baste it with sauce.
