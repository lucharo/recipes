## Ingredients

- 200g tofu
- Marinade
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder or one garlic clove
- 1 tsp smoked paprika powder
- 50g breadcrumbs

### How to

- Grind tofu, mix ingredients for the marinade together and combine with the tofu.
- 1 tortilla wrap
- 50g hummus
- veggies of choice
- fried tofu
- 20g Vfeta

## Method

1. To make it crispy
2. Add breadcrumbs and fry until brown and crispy.
3. Perfect for a wrap filling.
4. Wrap
5. Enjoy this beautiful recipe 😍
