## Ingredients

- 60g dark chocolate
- 2 tbsp coconut full fat cream solid
- 70g flour (GF optional)
- 25g cocoa powder
- 50g sugar granulated
- 100g oat milk
- 2 tsp apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp plant-based butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 50g whippable cream

## Method

1. Melt the chocolate and coconut cream together, stir to combine.
2. Once melted, freeze for 30 minutes.
3. Cake batter:
4. Preheat oven 190C/375F
5. Grease a muffin tray
6. Make buttermilk by combining oat milk and apple cider vinegar, set aside for 5 minutes.
7. Sift the flour and cocoa powder into a large bowl.
8. Add salt, sugar, baking powder, melted butter, buttermilk and vanilla extract.
9. Stir to combine - the batter should be thick, but pourable.
10. Pour the batter into the bottom of the ramekin or muffin tray, about 1/4 full.
11. Add 1 tbsp frozen chocolate ganache to the centre.
12. Cover with the remaining batter.
13. Bake for 15-17 minutes - edges are firm, but the inside jiggly.
14. Remove from oven, cool for 2-3 minutes.
15. Add the whippable cream to a bowl, whip until firm.
16. Gently wiggle a knife around the edges of the cake.
17. Serve cake with a sprinkling of icing sugar and cocoa powder and a dollop of whipped cream.
