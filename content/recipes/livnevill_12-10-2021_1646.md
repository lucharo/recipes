## Ingredients


### CHOCOLATE PEANUT BUTTER OATS WITH NUTTY CRUST 🍫

- 50g instant oats
- 30g vegan chocolate protein powder
- Tbsp cocoa powder
- 1/2tsp baking powder
- 140ml almond milk
- Sweetener of choice to taste

### For the nutty ‘crust’

- 1/2 an @adonis high protein peanut & chocolate bar (or sub for peanuts)
- 1tsp yoghurt
- Small splash of almond milk if needed

### To fill

- square of dark chocolate
- Tbsp peanut butter

### MACROS 🍫


## Method


### For the nutty ‘crust’

1. mix the oat ingredients together and 3/4 pop in a bowl
2. Place the peanut butter and chocolate on top, pour the remaining little bit of mixture on top, leaving room for the crust
3. Now mix together your nutty ‘crust’ ingredients till combined
4. then place this mixture over the top of the oats/ chocolate and peanut butter
5. bake in the oven for 20 mins at 170
