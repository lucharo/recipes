## Ingredients

- 300g pasta of choice
- 70 g pistachios (or more for a more prominent flavor)
- 30 g other nuts or seeds
- 2 handfuls of spinach
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1 garlic clove
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 handful of fresh basil
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- salt & pepper
- 1.  Toast the nuts and seeds until slightly browned from all sides.
- 669 kcal
- 27 g protein
- 71 g carbohydrates
- 28 g fats
- 16 g fiber

## Method

1. Blend all ingredients for the pesto/sauce. Blend it as fine or corse as you like.
2. Cook the pasta according to packaging.
3. Add the pesto/sauce to a pan, loosen with some pasta water, then add in the pasta.
4. Serve with some crushed pistachios and vegan parmesan - enjoy!
5. If you use a mortar you can adjust the consistency a bit more easily - the pesto is best when left a little more rough!
6. You can use any other nuts you like, walnuts, almonds, pine nuts - whatever you prefer!
