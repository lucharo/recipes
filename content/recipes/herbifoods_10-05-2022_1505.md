## Ingredients


### For the sauce

- 2 red peppers
- 3-4 medium tomatoes
- 1 eggplant
- 1 very small garlic bulb or 4 cloves
- salt and pepper
- 1-2 tbsp olive oil
- 50g cashews (or vegan cream)
- a splash of water, to blend
- 1-2 leeks
- fresh thyme or herbs of choice
- 300 g risotto rice
- 250 ml white wine
- vegetable broth, as needed
- vegan parmesan (optional)
- 58 g carbohydrates
- 13 g fats
- 9 g fiber

## Method


### For the sauce

1. Add peppers, tomatoes, eggplant and garlic to a baking tray. Sesaon with salt and pepper and drizzle with olive oil. Roast at 220°C for about 30 minutes.
2. In a pan sauté leek in a little bit of oil for 8-10 minutes, then add thyme and the risotto rice.
3. Deglaze the pan with white wine and gradually add in the broth, whenever needed.
4. When the veggies are done, blend with a handful of cashews and a splash of water until creamy.
5. When the risotto is almost done, add the veggies and cook for a few more minutes.
6. Serve with some vegan parmesan or however you prefer and enjoy!
