## Ingredients

- 2 Large portobello Mushrooms
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- 1/8 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp vegan butter
- 3 garlic cloves
- 4-6 sprigs of fresh thyme
- 3 sprigs of fresh rosemary
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

## Method

1. Rinse mushrooms well.
2. Dry off excess water with a cloth or paper towel.
3. In a little bowl, mix the salt, pepper, onion & garlic powder together and rub the mix on both sides of the mushrooms.
4. In a medium size pan bring the vegetable oil to medium heat.
5. Insert mushrooms and place a heavy object on top to help to release the moisture from the mushrooms.
6. Cook on each side for 3 minutes then add in the butter, garlic cloves, thyme and rosemary.
7. Base the mushrooms with the melted butter continuously for 30 seconds on each side.
8. Slice mushrooms into about 1-1.5 inch pieces and squeeze lemon juice on top.
9. Serve hot with rice, potatoes, in a sandwich or on a salad.

## Notes

Delivers amazing flavor and requires minimal time in the kitchen.

It’s juicy, smoky and great with rice, potatoes, inside a sandwich or on a salad.
